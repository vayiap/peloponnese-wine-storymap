# Digital Landscapes: Storymapping the Archaeology & Archaeometry of Wine in the Peloponnese

This repository contains the interactive **HTML storymap and digital poster** presented at the  
**9th Symposium for Archaeometry of the Hellenic Society of Archaeometry (HSA)**,  
University of the Peloponnese, Patra, November 2025.

## 🗺️ Overview

**Digital Landscapes of Wine in the Peloponnese** integrates archaeological, archaeobotanical, textual, and archaeometric datasets to explore the continuity of viticulture across southern Greece from the Early Bronze Age to the Ottoman period.  
The interactive map combines excavation reports, storage and production installations, vineyard areas, trade ports, and literary references to reconstruct the long history of wine in the Peloponnese.

The storymap was created using **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **[LeafletJS](https://leafletjs.com/)** for spatial visualization.

---

## 🌍 Features

- Interactive **LeafletJS** map showing archaeological sites, vineyards, presses, ports, and textual references.  
- Period and type filters for dynamic exploration.  
- Popup details for each location including site name, description, and category.  
- Fully responsive design for web and poster formats.  
- Warm parchment-inspired theme for digital-heritage readability.
